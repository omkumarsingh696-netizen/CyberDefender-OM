PHISHING AWARENESS

Red flags jo aapko pata hone chahiye

🔗 1. URL Red Flags

  • Spelling mistakes: instagarm.com, arnazon.com
  • Suspicious extensions: .xyz, .tk, .ml, .cf, .ga
  • Extra hyphens: instagram-login-verify.com
  • Subdomains: instagram.com.fake-site.xyz
  • Missing HTTPS: http:// instead of https://

📧 2. Email Red Flags

  • Urgency: "Account blocked in 24 hours"
  • Generic greeting: "Dear User" instead of name
  • Suspicious sender: support@amaz0n.com (zero)
  • Ask for password: Legit companies never ask
  • Suspicious links: Hover to see actual URL

💬 3. SMS/WhatsApp Red Flags

  • Lottery/Prize: "You won Rs 50,000!"
  • KYC update: "Update KYC or account blocked"
  • Forward message: "Forward to 10 people"
  • Unknown numbers: +91 98XXX from bank
  • Short links: bit.ly, tinyurl to hide real URL

🛡️ 4. How to Protect Yourself

  • ✅ Always check URL before entering password
  • ✅ Enable 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) everywhere
  • ✅ Use a password manager
  • ✅ Never share OTP with anyone
  • ✅ Banks never call/email asking for KYC
  • ✅ Report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in
  • ✅ When in doubt, DON'T click

🚨 5. If You Get Phished

  • 1. Immediately change your password
  • 2. Enable 2FA on all accounts
  • 3. Check bank statements for fraud
  • 4. Report to cybercrime.gov.in
  • 5. Call 1930 (Cyber Helpline India)
  • 6. Inform your bank immediately
